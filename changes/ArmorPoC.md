The armor system has been overhauled:
- There are now armor intrinsics, analogous to weapon attack patterns, taken from some armor runics and the ring of stealth
- Multiplicity runic and reprisal reworked to trigger off of a miss
The armor types are:
- Leather armor [3]<10> -- no intrinsic, does not generate in the dungeon like darts
- Hide armor [2]<10> -- stealth intrinsic, replaces ring of stealth
- Ring mail [5]<12> -- no intrinsic
- Scale mail [9]<14> -- armor bonus equal to 50% of net enchant
- Padded armor [7]<15> -- absorption intrinsic, absorption runic removed
- Spiked armor [8]<16> -- reprisal intrinsic, reprisal runic removed
- Mirror armor [9]<17> -- reflection intrinsic, reflection runic removed
- Plate armor [13]<19> -- no intrinsic, no runic
