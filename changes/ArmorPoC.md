The armor system has been overhauled:
- There are now armor passive effects, analogous to weapon attack patterns, taken from some armor runics and the ring of stealth
- Multiplicity armor effect reworked to trigger off of a miss
- Reprisal armor effect reworked to trigger off of a miss, renamed to "mangle" for better theming
The armor types are:
- Leather armor [3]<10>  -- no passive, high runic chance
- Hide armor    [4]<12>  -- stealth passive, extra armor above the base armor of 4 is halved, ring of stealth removed
- Scale mail    [7]<14>  -- armorsmith passive (+1 bonus armor when positively enchanted and additional bonus armor equal to 50% of raw enchant)
- Padded armor  [7]<15>  -- absorption passive, absorption runic removed
- Spiked armor  [8]<16>  -- mangle passive, reprisal runic removed
- Mirror armor  [9]<17>  -- reflection passive, reflection runic removed
