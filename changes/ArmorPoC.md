The armor system has been overhauled:
- There are now armor traits, analogous to weapon attack patterns, taken from some armor runics and the ring of stealth
- Multiplicity armor effect reworked to trigger off of a miss
- Reprisal armor effect reworked to trigger off of a miss, renamed to "mangle" for better theming
The armor types are:
- Leather armor [3]<10> -- no trait, starter item, no longer spawns in the dungeon
- Hide armor    [4]<12> -- stealth trait, extra armor above the base armor of 4 is halved, ring of stealth removed
- Scale mail    [7]<14> -- armorsmith trait (+1 bonus armor when positively enchanted and additional bonus armor equal to 50% of raw enchant)
- Padded armor  [7]<15> -- absorption trait, absorption runic removed
- Spiked armor  [8]<16> -- mangle trait, reprisal runic removed
- Mirror armor  [9]<17> -- reflection trait, reflection runic removed
